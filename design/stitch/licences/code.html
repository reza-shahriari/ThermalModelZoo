Strict Exclusion Criteria: What We Do NOT Accept

Assets submitted with restrictions on commercial application or modification are disqualified immediately during ingestion checks. Thermal radiometry simulation requires uninhibited geometric and parametric intervention.

cancel PROHIBITED

Non-Commercial (NC)

Licenses bearing CC-BY-NC or similar restrictions make datasets unusable for corporate labs, defense subcontractors, and dual-use aerospace sensor simulation.

Result: Auto-rejection at triage
cancel PROHIBITED

No-Derivatives (ND)

Thermal simulation mandates structural edits: polygon decimation, UV re-parameterization, thermal property assigning, and sub-assembly slicing. ND prevents baseline radiometric prep.

Result: Technically incompatible
cancel PROHIBITED

Generic 'Royalty-Free'

Generic commercial marketplace licenses (TurboSquid, CGTrader, Sketchfab Standard) typically restrict open dataset bundling, redistribution, and static web index embedding.

Result: Non-redistributable
cancel PROHIBITED

Unlicensed CAD Scrapes

Models ripped from proprietary flight simulators, military game archives, or GrabCAD without clear permissive open-access declarations are strictly forbidden.

Result: Legal risk zero-tolerance
science

Why NC/ND Invalidates Synthetic Thermal Datasets

In radiometric LWIR modeling, surfaces are assigned material conduction tensors and internal thermal loads. This mathematically creates a derivative 3D object, legally conflicting with No-Derivatives licenses.
