Thermal Metrology Protocol

What Makes IR Model Zoo Assets Different

Standard gaming meshes lack thermodynamic fidelity: single monolithic shells, non-physical gloss maps, and unverified licensing histories fail synthetic sensor tests.

hub
Pillar 01 / Geometry

Functional Part Decomposition

Every vehicle is disarticulated into its true heat-producing and heat-dissipating assemblies: internal combustion manifolds, electric motor coils, lithium-ion battery modules, brake calipers, and fluid heat-exchangers are distinct mesh elements.

check_circle Zero glued non-manifold shells
thermostat
Pillar 02 / Physics

Laboratory-Measured Emissivity

Surfaces carry calibrated physical LWIR (8–14 µm) spectral emissivity (ε) coefficients instead of arbitrary game-engine roughness textures. Direct ASTM E1311 compliant directional emissivity and thermal capacity parameters.

check_circle Standardized ε JSON sidecars
verified
Pillar 03 / Compliance

Explicit Provenance & Attribution

Every archive contains a machine-readable ATTRIBUTION.md and provenance chain. Only CC0 and CC-BY materials with audited 3D scanning or modeling lineages are accepted.
